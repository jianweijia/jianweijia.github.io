## Overview

This chip implements an ultralow-power, high-resolution, reconfigurable three-stage bandpass filter designed for intracardiac electrogram (IEGM) acquisition in implantable cardioverter defibrillators (ICDs). FeFET devices serve as programmable pseudo-high-value resistors (PHVRs) and capacitor structures.

## Key Results

- **Power**: 257 nW average power consumption
- **Gain tuning**: 17–77 dB
- **Bandwidth tuning**: 0.5–19 Hz (low cutoff), 23–138 Hz (high cutoff)
- **Resolution**: 11 µV minimum
